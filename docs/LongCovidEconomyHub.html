Methodology & Data Analysis

Our analysis utilizes the Proportional Reporting Ratio (PRR) from VAERS data to identify safety signals associated with Long COVID symptoms. By correlating these signals with national labor statistics, we estimate the potential scale of workforce disruption.

Why Long COVID Matters for Global GDP

As major economies face aging populations and labor shortages, the health of the remaining workforce is critical. Symptoms like Brain Fog, Chronic Fatigue, and Cognitive Disorder directly impact productivity and labor participation rates. Our data indicates that even a small percentage of affected workers can result in billions of dollars in lost economic output.
